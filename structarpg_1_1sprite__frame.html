◆ origin

vec2 arpg::sprite_frame::origin {}

Which point of the picture stands where the entity is, in pixels from its top left corner.

Not the centre by default: seen from above, a body is drawn taller than the ground it occupies, so what marks its place is its feet. Getting this wrong is only noticed once everything is drawn, and fixing it afterwards moves every sprite in the game at once.
