Detailed Description

A volley described by three figures rather than by a name.

Aimed shot, fan, circle and spiral are not four cases: they are what comes out of a count, an arc and a rotation per volley. A fan is an arc over several bullets, a circle is an arc of a full turn, a spiral is a single bullet with a spin. Naming them separately would have meant four code paths that cannot be combined.
