Detailed Description

What a data file describes: the archetypes, and the names they answer to.

Rooms and firing patterns will name the enemies they field rather than repeat their figures, which is what the names are kept for.

Empty when the load succeeded.

A catalogue carrying an error holds nothing: half a roster would field a wave nobody designed, and the mistake would show up as a strange fight rather than as a message.
